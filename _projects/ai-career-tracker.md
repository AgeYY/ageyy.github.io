Finding AI research opportunities often means searching across many employer websites. I built AI Career Tracker to bring postdoctoral and industry research and engineering positions together in one searchable board.

![AI Career Tracker showing its search, topic filters, and public job listings]({{ '/assets/img/projects/ai-career-tracker.png' | relative_url }}){: .img-fluid .rounded }

Browse opportunities in machine learning, large language models, multimodal AI, computer vision, agents, and robotics. Filter by research direction, role type, country, or workplace, then follow a listing to the original employer's posting. Source check dates help show how recently the listings were verified.

The public board is free to browse without an account. Coverage is selective, so check the original posting for current availability and requirements.
