About

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Neuroscience at The University of Texas at Austin, working with Prof. Xue-Xin Wei (2026–present). I received my Ph.D. in Physics from Washington University in St. Louis, where I studied computational neuroscience under the supervision of Prof. Ralf Wessel (2021–2026).

Prior to joining WashU, I worked with Prof. Changsong Zhou at Hong Kong Baptist University (2020–2021). Before that, I received my undergraduate training in physics at Sichuan University (2016–2020).

I also build small tools and explore research ideas for fun—see my projects.
